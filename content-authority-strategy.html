The service

What Content Optimization means

Content Optimization makes sure each page answers what people actually want to know, in a way search engines can understand. It often starts with updating existing pages, then adds new content where your site has gaps.

Authority comes from being useful and being recognised. We plan content around your expertise, link it sensibly across your site and support it with trustworthy mentions, so your Organic Traffic Growth is built on a solid base.

What is included

What we do

Typical work includes:

Content audit

Reviewing which pages perform, which need work and which can be merged or removed.

Topic and keyword planning

A plan of topics grouped by customer need and search intent.

Competitor analysis

Finding content gaps and opportunities competitors have already proven.

Content briefs

Clear briefs that tell writers what each page should cover and how to structure it.

Optimization of existing pages

Improving titles, headings, depth, internal links and clarity.

Authority building

Author profiles, expert content and direction for digital PR and mentions.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Content Optimization?

Content Optimization is improving existing and planned content so it better matches what people search for, answers their questions fully and is structured clearly for search engines.

Do you write the content?

We can create content briefs and optimize existing pages. Where writing is needed we can help, with a focus on accuracy and usefulness rather than volume.

How does content help Organic Traffic Growth?

Each useful page can attract searches your site does not currently capture. Over time, a well-planned set of pages builds relevance and trust that supports Organic Traffic Growth.

What is competitor analysis for content?

We review which topics and formats competitors rank for, find gaps and decide where you can realistically compete.

Do you use AI to write content?

We use tools to assist research and drafting where it helps, but content is reviewed for accuracy and usefulness, and we never publish low-quality filler.
