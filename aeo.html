What this service can include

Scope is tailored to your website, market, and goals. Typical work may include:

  • Audience-question and search-intent research.
  • Clear answer-first content structure with useful context.
  • FAQ and supporting content planning when appropriate.
  • Schema markup review based on visible page content and eligibility.
  • Content improvements for clarity, trust, and accessibility.

Our process

  1. Collect real customer questions and search queries.
  2. Map questions to the right pages and content formats.
  3. Write and organize accurate, concise answers with supporting detail.
  4. Review engagement and search data to improve the content.

What to expect

You receive clear priorities and recommendations you can understand and act on. Results vary by industry, competition, implementation, website history, and other factors; rankings or AI citations cannot be guaranteed.
