The service

What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization (GEO), also called generative SEO, shapes your website and content so AI tools can understand who you are, what you offer and why you can be trusted.

More people now ask AI assistants for advice and recommendations instead of only scrolling search results, and those assistants write answers using information from across the web. GEO builds on the same foundation as good SEO: clear pages, accurate information and recognised expertise. We add the extra layer that helps AI systems quote you correctly: explicit facts, consistent business details and structured data.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of making your content and website easier for generative AI tools, such as AI overviews and chat assistants, to understand, trust and reference when they answer questions about your market.

What is generative SEO?

Generative SEO is another name for the same idea: adapting your SEO so that AI systems can understand your business and use your information in the answers they write. It builds on traditional SEO rather than replacing it.

What is the difference between generative engine optimization and traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO helps your pages rank in lists of search results. GEO helps AI systems summarize and cite your information in generated answers. Clear, accurate and well-organized content helps both, so we treat them together.

How are GEO, SEO and AEO different?

SEO focuses on rankings, AEO on being the direct answer to specific questions, and GEO on how generative AI tools describe and reference your business overall. They overlap, and many projects combine all three.

Is GEO worth it for a small business?

For many, yes, because more buyers now ask AI tools for recommendations before they search. The work is largely good SEO practice done carefully, so it supports your existing efforts rather than competing with them.

How much do affordable generative engine optimization services cost?

It depends on your site, your market and how much needs fixing. After a short review we recommend a focused scope and explain what is included, so you pay for the work you need. We do not publish fixed prices.

Can you guarantee my business will appear in AI answers?

No. AI tools choose sources in ways no one fully controls. We improve the factors you can control: clear content, accurate business information, structured data and trusted mentions, and we report honestly on what changes.

What do generative AI SEO services involve?

They typically involve checking how AI tools describe your business, fixing missing or wrong information, improving content structure, adding structured data and monitoring results over time.
