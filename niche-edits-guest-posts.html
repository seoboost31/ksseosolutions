The service

Niche edits and guest posts in plain terms

Niche edits and guest posts are two ways to earn backlinks from websites in your industry. A guest post is a new article written for another site. A niche edit adds a link to an article that is already live. Both work best when the website is relevant, the content is useful and the link makes sense to the reader.

Links from trusted, relevant sites are one of the signals search engines use to judge a website, which is why this work sits alongside our link building and website SEO services. We work remotely and handle the research, outreach and writing, and you approve each placement. Rankora Digital Agency works remotely with businesses across the United States, including Denver, Colorado.

Site quality

How we choose websites

Most of the risk in link building comes from where the links are placed. We look at whether the site has real readers, publishes useful content on a clear topic and is not selling links to anyone who asks. A good site has genuine articles, a visible audience and links to other sites that make sense.

We avoid private blog networks, sites with no traffic and pages with dozens of unrelated outbound links. Metrics such as Ahrefs Domain Rating help us compare sites, but relevance and real readers matter more than any score.

Google guidelines

Staying inside Google's rules

Google's spam policies cover link schemes, including buying links to pass ranking signals. Where a link is paid, Google asks that it be marked with a rel="sponsored" attribute. We choose editorial placements, tell you plainly how each site works and recommend the marking Google asks for when it applies.

No agency can promise that every link will count the same way or stay live forever. If a link we placed is removed, we tell you.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What are niche edits?

A niche edit, also called a link insertion, adds a link to your page inside an article that is already published on a relevant website. The article already has readers and some history, so the link sits in useful context instead of on a brand-new page.

What is a guest post?

A guest post is an original article you write for another website in your industry. It gives that site's readers something useful and earns your business a link and a mention, usually in the article or the author bio.

What is the difference between niche edits and guest posts?

Niche edits add a link to content that already exists, so they are quicker. Guest posts are new articles written for a specific site, so they take longer but let you cover a topic in depth. Many projects use a mix of both.

Are guest posts and niche edits safe for SEO?

They can be, if the sites are relevant, have real readers and the links are placed naturally. Google's spam policies target links made to manipulate rankings, such as link farms and undisclosed paid links, so we avoid those and choose sites on quality and relevance.

Do you use private blog networks?

No. We do not use private blog networks, link farms or sites that exist only to sell links. We pick real websites with an audience in your field and show you each one before anything is published.

How do you choose websites for guest posts and niche edits?

We look at topic relevance first, then real organic traffic, content quality, how the site treats outbound links and whether it looks like a genuine publication. Third-party scores such as Ahrefs Domain Rating are one input, not the goal.

How much do guest posts and niche edits cost?

The cost depends on the sites, the number of links and whether we write new content. After we understand your goals we suggest a small set of sites and explain what each placement involves, so you can approve it before we start.

How long until I see results?

A new link can be found by Google within days or weeks, but its effect on rankings usually builds over months and depends on your competition and your site. We do not guarantee rankings or traffic.
