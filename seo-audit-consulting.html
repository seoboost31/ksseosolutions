The service

What a Website SEO Audit tells you

A Website SEO Audit is the fastest way to understand why a site is not performing and what will help most. We review the technical setup, on-page content, structure, search visibility and competitors, then turn the findings into a plan ordered by impact.

Many clients hire a Professional SEO Consultant because they have a site but no clear direction. The audit gives you that direction, and consulting lets you keep an experienced second opinion as you act on it.

What is included

What the audit covers

Typical work includes:

Technical checks

Crawling, indexation, speed, mobile usability, redirects and errors.

On-page review

Titles, headings, content quality, keyword targeting and internal links.

Content gap analysis

Topics and searches competitors cover that you do not.

Competitor review

How the sites ranking for your target searches are built and where you can compete.

Local and GBP check

Business listing and profile consistency where relevant.

Prioritized action plan

Findings ranked by impact and effort, written so you or your developer can act.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is a Website SEO Audit?

A Website SEO Audit is a structured review of your site's technical health, on-page content, structure and competitors, ending with a prioritized list of fixes.

What will I receive?

A clear report with findings in plain language, a prioritized action plan and a walkthrough so you know what to do first. We can also implement the fixes if you wish.

How long does an audit take?

It depends on the size and complexity of your site. After seeing your website we give you an estimate before we begin.

Can you work with our developer?

Yes. We write recommendations in a way a developer can act on, and we can answer their questions.

What does SEO consulting include?

Consulting is ongoing guidance: reviewing plans, answering questions, checking work done by your team or other vendors and helping you prioritize. It suits teams that want expert advice without a full retainer.

Can I hire a Remote SEO expert for a short project?

Yes. Audits and consulting are common short engagements. You can continue with ongoing work later if it makes sense.
