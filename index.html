Before you hire

Questions to ask before you hire an SEO Agency

Whichever provider you consider, these questions help you compare them fairly.

  • What will you change on my website, and why?
  • How do you prioritize work for a small budget?
  • How often will I get reports, and what will they show?
  • Who will do the work, and who will I speak with?
  • How do you approach Link Building and Google’s guidelines?
Know what to avoid

Red flags to watch for

  • Guaranteed rankings or “page one in 30 days” promises.
  • No clear explanation of what work will be done.
  • Reports that show only vanity numbers.
  • Paid link networks, copied content or other shortcuts.
  • Long contracts with an unclear scope.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term channel. Many websites see early movement within a few months, but timing varies with competition, website history and how quickly changes are implemented. We do not guarantee rankings; we track Organic Traffic Growth and search visibility and report what the data shows.

Does SEO really work for small businesses?

Yes, when it is matched to the right goals. SEO helps customers who are already searching find your business, which can make it a cost-effective channel for a small company. Results take time and depend on competition, so we set realistic expectations from the start.

What is the difference between Local SEO and regular SEO?

Local SEO helps your business appear for searches tied to a place, using accurate business information, location-focused pages and Google Business Profile Optimization where eligible. Regular SEO focuses on how your website performs in organic search more broadly. Many small businesses benefit from both.

How do buyers find small businesses in AI search?

No one can guarantee a place in AI answers, but these steps support both traditional rankings and AI search.
