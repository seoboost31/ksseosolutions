The service

Working with a local SEO specialist

When someone searches for a service in their area, Google shows a map and a short list of local businesses. A local SEO specialist helps your business earn a place there by making your business information accurate, your profile complete and your website clear about what you do and where you serve.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does a local SEO specialist do?

A local SEO specialist helps your business appear when people search for services near them. The work includes Google Business Profile optimization, local keyword targeting, location and service pages, consistent business listings and review strategy.

Can a remote agency do local SEO for my city?

Yes. Most local SEO work is done online: your Google Business Profile, website, listings and content. We work remotely with businesses across the United States, including Denver, Colorado. We never create a fake or virtual address for a profile.

Do I need a physical location?

It depends. Google Business Profile has rules: a business generally needs a real storefront or must serve customers in person within a service area. We review your situation and tell you honestly what you are eligible for.

What is the difference between local SEO and regular SEO?

Local SEO focuses on searches tied to a place, such as "plumber near me". Regular SEO focuses on organic performance more broadly. Many small businesses benefit from both.

How long does local SEO take?

Some improvements to a profile or listings can show within weeks, while stronger local rankings usually take months and depend on competition. We do not guarantee map pack positions.
