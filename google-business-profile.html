Google Business Profile Optimization for Local Businesses

We set up, fix and manage your Google Business Profile, so people searching in Google Search and Google Maps can find you, trust you and get in touch.

The service

What Google Business Profile optimization includes

Your Google Business Profile is the free listing that shows your business in Google Search and Google Maps, with your phone number, hours, photos and reviews. Google Business Profile optimization makes that listing complete, accurate and consistent with your website, so it can compete in the local results.

It is the part of local SEO that people see first. When someone searches for a service nearby, the profile often decides whether they call you, ask for directions or choose another business. Because we work remotely, we handle the setup and updates online and ask you only for the details only you know, such as your real services and photos. Rankora Digital Agency works remotely with businesses across the United States, including Denver, Colorado.

Scope depends on your business and what your profile needs most. Typical work includes:

Ranking in Google Maps

What affects Google Maps results

Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. Relevance is how well your profile matches the search. Distance is how far you are from the searcher, which no agency can change. Prominence is how well known and trusted your business is, which comes from reviews, listings, links and your website.

We work on the parts you can influence and track them on a local rank grid, which shows where you appear in Google Maps from different points across your area. See how that looks on our case studies page, and read more about local SEO.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Google Business Profile optimization?

Google Business Profile optimization means completing and maintaining your profile so Google understands your business and customers can trust it. It covers categories, services, hours, photos, posts, reviews and the details that match your website and listings.

Is Google Business Profile the same as Google My Business?

Yes. Google renamed Google My Business to Google Business Profile. Customers now manage it directly in Google Search and Google Maps, and the free listing is the same one that shows your business in local results.

How do I rank higher in Google Maps?

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence. You cannot change distance, but you can improve relevance with the right categories and services, and prominence with reviews, consistent listings and a clear website. Rankings are never guaranteed.

How much does Google Business Profile management cost?

It depends on how many locations you have, how much needs fixing and how often you want updates. After reviewing your profile we recommend a focused scope and explain what is included, so you can compare it with other options.

Can I use a virtual office or P.O. box as my address?

No. Google only allows a real address where your business can meet customers, and it does not accept P.O. boxes or most virtual offices. If you travel to customers, you can hide your address and set a service area instead. We will not create a fake address for you.

Do you help if my profile is suspended?

Yes, we can review why it was suspended, correct the issue and help prepare the reinstatement request. Google makes the final decision, so we cannot promise the outcome or how long it takes.

Can you get me more Google reviews?

We set up a simple, compliant way to ask your real customers for reviews and help you reply to them. We do not buy reviews, write fake ones or filter who is allowed to leave one, because that breaks Google's rules and the reviews can be removed.

How long does Google Business Profile optimization take to work?

Some fixes, such as the right category or a corrected phone number, can show within weeks. Stronger visibility usually takes months and depends on competition. We do not guarantee rankings or lead numbers.
