The service

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Many searches end with an answer instead of a click: a featured snippet, a voice assistant reply or an AI summary. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is about making your content the clearest, most trustworthy source for the questions your customers ask.

That means writing direct answers, organizing pages around real questions and describing your content in a way machines can read. It is closely related to AI SEO and GEO, with a sharper focus on questions and answers.

What is included

What AEO work covers

Typical work includes:

Question research

Finding the questions customers ask before, during and after buying.

Answer-first content

Short, direct answers at the top of sections, supported by detail below.

FAQ planning

Useful FAQ sections on the right pages, not filler.

Structured data

FAQ, how-to and business markup that matches the visible content.

Page structure

Question-style headings, lists and tables that are easy to extract.

Tracking

Monitoring featured snippets, "people also ask" visibility and organic traffic.

Honest expectations

AEO improves clarity for readers and machines alike. Appearing as a featured answer depends on competition and search engine choices, so we do not guarantee placements. We do aim to leave you with clearer, more useful pages.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

AEO is optimizing content so search engines and AI assistants can pull a direct, accurate answer from your pages, as in featured snippets, voice answers and AI summaries.

How is AEO different from GEO?

AEO focuses on being the direct answer to specific questions. GEO focuses on how generative AI systems understand and reference your business overall. They work well together and we often combine them.

Does AEO replace SEO?

No. AEO builds on SEO. Pages still need to be crawlable, relevant and trustworthy before they can be chosen as an answer.

What questions should my site answer?

We research the questions your customers really ask, such as costs, comparisons, how-to steps and "is it worth it" queries, then answer them clearly on the right pages.

Can you guarantee featured snippets?

No. Search engines decide what to show. We structure your content so it is a strong candidate and measure how often it appears.
