Before you hire

Questions to ask before you hire an SEO agency

Whichever provider you consider, these questions help you compare them fairly.

  • What will you change on my website, and why?
  • How do you prioritize work for a small budget?
  • How often will I get reports, and what will they show?
  • Who will do the work, and who will I speak with?
  • How do you approach link building and Google’s guidelines?
Know what to avoid

Red flags to watch for

  • Guaranteed rankings or “page one in 30 days” promises.
  • No clear explanation of what work will be done.
  • Reports that show only vanity numbers.
  • Paid link networks, copied content or other shortcuts.
  • Long contracts with an unclear scope.

Small business SEO questions

Frequently asked questions

There is no single SEO package that suits every organization. The right scope depends on your starting point and the customers you want to reach.

What do SEO services for small business include?

A well-rounded plan may include keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, local SEO and Google Business Profile optimization, content optimization, link building and SEO performance tracking. The right mix depends on your website, your market and your goals.

How much do small business SEO services cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your website, your competition and the scope of work. After reviewing your website and goals, we recommend a focused plan and explain what is included, so you can choose affordable small business SEO services that fit your budget.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term channel. Many websites see early movement within a few months, but timing varies with competition, website history and how quickly changes are implemented. We do not guarantee rankings; we track organic traffic growth and search visibility and report what the data shows.

What is the difference between local SEO and regular SEO?

Local SEO helps your business appear for searches tied to a place, using accurate business information, location-focused pages and Google Business Profile optimization where eligible. Regular SEO focuses on how your website performs in organic search more broadly. Many small businesses benefit from both.

Do you help with AI search, GEO and AEO?

Yes. We organize your content so important information is clear to people and to AI-powered search experiences, including Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO). No one can guarantee citations in AI answers, so we focus on clarity, accuracy and useful content.

Can you support startups, B2B companies and international markets?

Our SEO services for startups and B2B SEO services follow the same process: understand your audience, prioritize the work and improve your website step by step. Because we work remotely, we can also discuss international SEO if you serve more than one country. Share your markets and we will recommend an approach.
